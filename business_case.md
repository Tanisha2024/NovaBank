Project:
NovaBank – Credit Risk, Fraud & Collections Intelligence System

Business:
A fictional digital lending/fintech company.

Business Problem:
NovaBank wants to improve lending decisions, identify suspicious
transactions and prioritize collections activity using historical data.

Stakeholders:
1. Credit Risk Manager
2. Fraud Manager
3. Collections Manager
4. Senior Management

Key Decisions:
1. Which borrowers represent higher credit risk?
2. What lending threshold balances growth and expected loss?
3. Which transactions should investigators review first?
4. Which overdue customers should collections prioritize?
5. What portfolio segments require management intervention?

Success Criteria:
The project should produce measurable analytical evidence
showing whether the proposed strategies improve decision quality
compared with simple baseline strategies.

## AI Layer

The AI layer will assist analysts and managers rather than replace
the underlying analytical models.

AI capabilities:

1. Risk Explanation
   Convert model outputs and SHAP factors into a concise,
   human-readable risk explanation.

2. Fraud Investigation Copilot
   Summarize suspicious transactions and relevant behavioural
   evidence for analyst review.

3. Collections Copilot
   Summarize customer repayment history, risk and outstanding
   exposure to assist the collections team.

4. Natural-Language Analytics
   Allow users to ask questions about verified portfolio metrics
   and receive evidence-based answers.

Important:
The LLM will not independently approve loans, declare a transaction
fraudulent, or make financial decisions. It will operate on verified
data and model outputs.

Important Limitation:
NovaBank is a simulated business case. No real financial impact
will be claimed. Impact will be demonstrated through historical
backtesting/simulation.
